Reimbursement & Printing Tips

Quick best practices to ensure your printed claims are accepted without hassle.

Print Setup in Browser

Press Ctrl + P. In Chrome or Edge, expand More settings and check Background graphics so barcodes, tables, and brand blocks print clearly.

100% Private & Local

All calculations, barcodes, and layouts run purely inside your local browser. No financial data, vehicle numbers, or billing info is ever uploaded to any server.

Thermal Slip Sizing

The Fuel Receipt Studio generates receipts at 256px width, mimicking 58mm standard POS roll paper. Cut margins are auto-calculated for realistic expense proofs.
